🔍 3-Bo'lim: 10 ta Klassik Qopqon & Xatolar Tahlili

Boshlang'ich o'quvchilar eng ko'p qiladigan xatolar va ularning qat'iy qoidalari.

❌ She have two brothers and one sister.
✅ She has two brothers and one sister.
💡 Qoida tushuntirishi: 'She' uchun 'have' emas, 'has' ishlatiladi.
❌ I don't can play tennis on Sundays.
✅ I can't play tennis on Sundays.
💡 Qoida tushuntirishi: 'can' modal fe'li 'don't' olmaydi, inkor shakli 'can't'.
❌ My father is doctor in a big hospital.
✅ My father is a doctor in a big hospital.
💡 Qoida tushuntirishi: Kasb-hunarlar oldidan birlikda 'a' yoki 'an' artikli qo'yilishi shart.
❌ Yesterday, we was very tired after work.
✅ Yesterday, we were very tired after work.
💡 Qoida tushuntirishi: 'we' ko'plik, o'tgan zamonda 'were' bo'ladi.
❌ He doesn't likes watching horror movies.
✅ He doesn't like watching horror movies.
💡 Qoida tushuntirishi: 'doesn't' yordamchi fe'lidan keyin asosiy fe'ldagi -s tushib qoladi: 'like'.
❌ She is more tall than her mother.
✅ She is taller than her mother.
💡 Qoida tushuntirishi: Bir bo'g'inli sifatlarga 'more' qo'shilmaydi, '-er' qo'shiladi: 'taller'.
❌ Did you went to the cinema last night?
✅ Did you go to the cinema last night?
💡 Qoida tushuntirishi: 'Did' kelgan savolda fe'l V1 shakliga qaytadi: 'Did you go'?
❌ There is many books on the desk.
✅ There are many books on the desk.
💡 Qoida tushuntirishi: 'many books' ko'plik, shuning uchun 'There are' bo'ladi.
❌ I am agree with your opinion.
✅ I agree with your opinion.
💡 Qoida tushuntirishi: 'agree' fe'l (rozi bo'lmoq), u bilan 'am' ishlatilmaydi.
❌ She walked home while it was rain.
✅ She walked home while it was raining.
💡 Qoida tushuntirishi: Past Continuous da 'was' dan keyin -ing bo'lishi shart: 'was raining'.

📖 4-Bo'lim: Matn Tushunish Qobiliyati (Reading)

Matnlarni diqqat bilan o'qing va savollarga javob bering.

📖 1-Matn: Anna's Daily Routine (A0–A1 Level)

Anna is 22 years old and she lives in Tashkent. She is an English teacher at a language center. Every day, Anna gets up at 7:00 AM. She has a cup of green tea and eats toast with cheese for breakfast. At 8:15 AM, she takes the bus to work. She teaches four classes every day. In the evening, Anna cooks dinner with her mother and reads books. On weekends, she likes riding her bicycle in the park with her friends. She loves her job because she enjoys helping young learners.

Savol #1: What does Anna do for a living?
Savol #2: What does she eat for breakfast?
Savol #3: How does she travel to work?
Savol #4: What does she do on weekends?
📖 2-Matn: An Adventure in the Mountains (A1–A2 Bridge)

Last Saturday, Bobur and his friends decided to go hiking in the Chimgan mountains. The weather was sunny and warm when they left the city at 8:00 AM. While they were walking up the green hill, they saw beautiful wild birds and colorful flowers. At around 1:00 PM, they stopped near a river to eat lunch. Suddenly, dark clouds appeared in the sky and heavy rain started to fall. Luckily, Bobur had waterproof jackets in his backpack. They found a small shelter and waited there until the rain stopped. It was an exciting and unforgettable adventure for all of them.

Savol #5: Where did Bobur and his friends go last Saturday?
Savol #6: What were they doing when they saw wild birds?
Savol #7: What happened while they were near the river?
Savol #8: Why were they lucky when it rained?
