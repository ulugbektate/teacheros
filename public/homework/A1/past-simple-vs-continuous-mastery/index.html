Past Continuous vs Past Simple: Mukammal Tekshiruv & Amaliyot

O'tgan oddiy zamon (tugallangan harakatlar) va o'tgan davomli zamon (jarayon, to'satdan bo'lgan hodisalar)ni farqlash, 30 ta asosiy noto'g'ri fe'lni mustahkamlash va 50 ta yangi so'zni faol o'zlashtirish bo'yicha maxsus vazifa.

📖 Dars Qoidalari & Oltin Formulalar
Past Simple va Past Continuous o'rtasidagi asosiy farqlarni bir qarashda o'rganing.
⚡ 1. Past Simple (O'tgan Oddiy)
Subject + V2 (ed / irregular)

Mohiyati: O'tmishda tugallangan, aniq nuqtadagi qisqa harakatlar yoki ketma-ket sodir bo'lgan voqealar.

✅ I woke up, brushed my teeth and left the house.
✅ Yesterday at 3 PM, she called me.
⏱️ 2. Past Continuous (O'tgan Davomli)
was / were + Verb-ing

Mohiyati: O'tmishdagi ma'lum bir daqiqada davom etayotgan jarayon yoki fon holati.

✅ At 8:00 PM yesterday, I was studying English.
✅ Heavy rain was falling all night.
🔗 3. When & While Bog'lovchilari
While + Continuous | When + Simple

Qoida: Uzoq fon jarayoni oldidan While, to'satdan bo'lgan qisqa harakat oldidan When keladi.

✅ While I was cooking, the phone rang.
✅ I was walking home when I saw an accident.

⚠️ O'zbek O'quvchilari Uchun 3 Katta Tuzoq:

  • 1-tuzoq: "I was cook" ❌ ➔ "I was cooking" ✅ (was/were dan keyin har doim -ing bo'lishi shart!).
  • 2-tuzoq: Inkor shaklida "I didn't was" ❌ ➔ "I wasn't" yoki "I didn't go" ✅ (ikkalasini aralashtirib yubormang!).
  • 3-tuzoq: His-tuyg'u va holat fe'llari (stative verbs: know, want, hear, understand, like) Continuous zamonda ISHLATILMAYDI: "I was knowing him" ❌ ➔ "I knew him" ✅.

🔍 7. Xatolarni Tahlil Qilish (10 ta Tuzoq)
O'zbek o'quvchilari eng ko'p qiladigan 10 ta xatoni ko'rib chiqing va nima uchun noto'g'ri ekanini o'rganing.
❌ I was watch TV when she called me.
✅ I was watching TV when she called me.
💡 Izoh: XATO: 'was' dan keyin fe'lga albatta -ing qo'shilishi shart: 'was watching'.
❌ While he arrived at school, the bell was ringing.
✅ When he arrived at school, the bell was ringing.
💡 Izoh: XATO: 'arrived' qisqa harakat. Qisqa harakatlar oldidan 'While' emas, 'When' qo'yiladi.
❌ We was walking home yesterday at 5 PM.
✅ We were walking home yesterday at 5 PM.
💡 Izoh: XATO: 'We' ko'plik shaxs, unga 'was' emas, 'were' ishlatiladi: 'We were walking'.
❌ She didn't was listening to the teacher.
✅ She wasn't listening to the teacher.
💡 Izoh: XATO: Past Continuous inkorida 'didn't was' bo'lmaydi! Faqat 'wasn't' yoki 'weren't'.
❌ Yesterday, I saw an accident and was calling the police.
✅ Yesterday, I saw an accident and called the police.
💡 Izoh: XATO: Ketma-ket sodir bo'lgan voqealar ikkalasi ham Past Simple bo'ladi: 'saw ... and called'.
❌ What did you doing yesterday at 8 o'clock?
✅ What were you doing yesterday at 8 o'clock?
💡 Izoh: XATO: Aniq vaqtdagi jarayon uchun 'did you doing' emas, 'were you doing' ishlatiladi.
❌ While I walked in the garden, I found a gold coin.
✅ While I was walking in the garden, I found a gold coin.
💡 Izoh: XATO: 'While' odatda uzoq davom etgan jarayon (Past Continuous: 'was walking') bilan keladi.
❌ It rained heavily all night yesterday.
✅ It was raining heavily all night yesterday.
💡 Izoh: XATO: 'all night' (butun tun bo'yi) davomiylikni bildiradi: 'was raining heavily'.
❌ He dropped his phone while he was ran.
✅ He dropped his phone while he was running.
💡 Izoh: XATO: 'was' dan keyin V2 ('ran') emas, V-ing ('running') kelishi shart.
❌ Did you were sleeping when the door knocked?
✅ Were you sleeping when the door knocked?
💡 Izoh: XATO: Past Continuous so'rog'ida 'Did' ishlatilmaydi: 'Were you sleeping...?'
