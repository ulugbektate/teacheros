Past Continuous: When, While & So

O'tgan davomli zamon va o'tgan oddiy zamon orasidagi farq, to'satdan bo'lgan harakatlar va natijalarni ifodalash bo'yicha to'liq mustahkamlash vazifasi.

📖 Dars Qoidalari & O'zbek Tili Tuzoqlari

Past Continuous (o'tgan davomli zamon) va Past Simple (o'tgan oddiy zamon) formulasini eslab qoling.

⏱️ Past Continuous (was/were + V-ing)

O'tmishdagi ma'lum bir vaqtda davom etayotgan (uzoq) jarayonni bildiradi.
• I / He / She / It → was + V-ing
• We / You / They → were + V-ing
At 8 PM yesterday, I was studying English.

⚡ Past Simple (V2 / -ed)

O'tmishda tugallangan, qisqa, to'satdan sodir bo'lgan yoki birin-ketin sodir bo'lgan harakatlar.
• Regular verbs: verb + ed (walked, played)
• Irregular verbs: V2 ustuni (went, saw, rang)
Suddenly, the phone rang.

🔗 When vs While vs So

• WHILE + Uzoq jarayon (Past Continuous): While I was cooking...
• WHEN + Qisqa to'sqinlik qiluvchi harakat (Past Simple): ...when the phone rang.
• SO + Natija (shuning uchun): It was raining, so we stayed home.
While I was walking, it rained, so I ran home.
⚠️ O'zbek Tili Tuzog'i #1: Holat fe'llari (Stative Verbs)
O'zbek tilida "Men eshitayotgan edim", "Men tushunayotgan edim" deyish mumkin. Ammo ingliz tilida sezgi va aqliy fe'llar (hear, see, understand, know, want) hech qachon Continuous bo'lmaydi!
❌ I was hearing a sound.   ➔   ✅ I heard a sound.
❌ She was knowing the answer.   ➔   ✅ She knew the answer.

💎 15 ta Yangi Mavzuviy So'z (Mavzuga Oid)

Kutilmagan hodisalar, to'satdan voqealar va Past Continuous konteksti uchun yangi so'zlar.

1. interrupt verb
gapini bo'lmoq, xalal bermoq
"The phone rang and interrupted our lesson."
2. slip verb
toyib ketmoq, sirpanmoq
"She slipped on the wet floor while she was walking."
3. burn verb
kuydirib olmoq / yonmoq
"He burned his finger while he was cooking soup."
4. notice verb
payqab qolmoq, sezmoq
"I was looking out the window when I noticed a strange car."
5. drop verb
tushirib yubormoq
"She dropped her cup while she was washing dishes."
6. ring verb (rang, rung)
jiringlamoq
"I was sleeping when the alarm rang loudly."
7. cross verb
(yo'lni) kesib o'tmoq
"A dog ran across the street while we were driving."
8. carry verb
ko'tarib bormoq, tashimoq
"He was carrying heavy bags, so I helped him."
9. spill verb
to'kib yubormoq (choy/suv)
"I was drinking tea when I spilled it on my book."
10. knock verb
taqillatmoq (eshikni)
"Someone knocked on the door while I was studying."
11. accident noun
baxtsiz hodisa, to'qnashuv
"There was a small car accident on the main road."
12. loud adj
baland, jarangdor (ovoz)
"We heard a loud noise while we were talking."
13. scream verb
qichqirmoq, baqirmoq
"The boy screamed when he saw a big spider."
14. sudden adj
to'satdan, kutilmagan
"A sudden noise woke me up at 3 AM."
15. quietly adv
sekin, ovoz chiqarmay, sokin
"She was reading quietly while her baby was sleeping."

✏️ Qavslarni Ochish (15 ta Mashq)

Fe'lni to'g'ri shaklda yozing: Past Continuous (was/were + V-ing) yoki Past Simple (V2).

Mashq #1 Maslahat: Mary (she) + was + cook-ing
While Mary ______ (cook) dinner, her brother arrived home.
Mashq #2 Maslahat: To'satdan o'chdi (go -> went)
I was reading a book when the lights suddenly ______ (go) out.
Mashq #3 Maslahat: They + were + watch-ing
They ______ (watch) television when someone knocked on the door.
Mashq #4 Maslahat: I + was + walk-ing
While I ______ (walk) in the park, it started to rain.
Mashq #5 Maslahat: He + was + cross-ing
He slipped and fell while he ______ (cross) the icy road.
Mashq #6 Maslahat: The students (ko'plik) + were + writing
The students ______ (write) a test when the director entered the classroom.
Mashq #7 Maslahat: Qisqa to'satdan voqea: burned yoki burnt
I ______ (burn) my hand while I was ironing the shirt.
Mashq #8 Maslahat: Zilzila yuz berdi (Past Simple): happened
What were you doing when the earthquake ______ (happen)?
Mashq #9 Maslahat: Sarah + was + sleep-ing
Sarah ______ (sleep) quietly when her alarm clock rang.
Mashq #10 Maslahat: We + were + wait-ing
While we ______ (wait) for the bus, we saw an accident.
Mashq #11 Maslahat: Qisqa oqibat (break -> broke)
He dropped his phone and it ______ (break) into pieces.
Mashq #12 Maslahat: My parents (they) + were + having
My parents ______ (have) breakfast when I woke up.
Mashq #13 Maslahat: Natija harakati (Past Simple): walked
She was carrying hot tea, so she ______ (walk) very carefully.
Mashq #14 Maslahat: The birds (ko'plik) + were + sing-ing
At 7:00 yesterday morning, the birds ______ (sing) outside my window.
Mashq #15 Maslahat: Topib oldim (find -> found)
I was looking at old photos when I ______ (find) my grandmother's letter.

🔗 When / While / So Bog'lovchilari (10 ta Mashq)

Nuqtalar o'rniga when, while yoki so so'zlaridan mosini qo'ying.

Bog'lovchi #1 Ikkita uzoq jarayon bir vaqtda sodir bo'lyapti (While)
______ I was doing my homework, my brother was listening to music.
Bog'lovchi #2 To'satdan topib oldim (qisqa harakat oldidan: when)
I was cleaning the room ______ I found my lost ring.
Bog'lovchi #3 Sho'rva qaynayotgan edi, SHUNING UCHUN olovni pasaytirdi (so)
The soup was boiling, ______ she turned down the gas stove.
Bog'lovchi #4 Idish yuvayotgan paytida (jarayon oldidan: while)
She dropped the plate ______ she was washing the dishes.
Bog'lovchi #5 Yomg'ir to'satdan yog'a boshladi (when)
We were having a picnic ______ it started to rain heavily.
Bog'lovchi #6 Chiptani yo'qotib qo'ydim, shuning uchun piyoda ketishga to'g'ri keldi (so)
I lost my bus ticket, ______ I had to walk all the way home.
Bog'lovchi #7 O'qituvchi tushuntirayotgan paytda (jarayon boshida: While)
______ the teacher was explaining the rule, nobody said a word.
Bog'lovchi #8 Tez haydayotgan edi, SHUNING UCHUN to'xtata olmadi (so)
He was driving fast, ______ he couldn't stop the car in time.
Bog'lovchi #9 Ovqatlanayotgan paytimizda (jarayon: while)
The phone rang three times ______ we were eating dinner.
Bog'lovchi #10 To'satdan mushuk kesib o'tdi (qisqa harakat oldidan: when)
He was riding his bicycle ______ a black cat crossed the street.

🔍 Xatolarni Tahlil Qilish (10 ta Tuzoq)

Har bir gapdagi odatiy xatolarni ko'rib chiqing va nima uchun noto'g'riligini o'rganing.

❌ Xato: "While I saw him, he was running to the bus stop."
✅ To'g'ri: "When I saw him, he was running to the bus stop. (yoki: While he was running, I saw him.)"
💡 Qoida: 'See' qisqa harakat, shuning uchun 'While I saw' emas, 'When I saw' yoki 'While he was running' bo'ladi.
❌ Xato: "I was watching TV when my friend was calling me."
✅ To'g'ri: "I was watching TV when my friend called me."
💡 Qoida: 'When' dan keyin to'satdan sodir bo'lgan qisqa harakat keladi: 'called' (Past Simple), 'was calling' emas.
❌ Xato: "They was playing tennis yesterday at 5 PM."
✅ To'g'ri: "They were playing tennis yesterday at 5 PM."
💡 Qoida: 'They' ko'plik olmoshi, shuning uchun 'was' emas, 'were' bo'lishi shart.
❌ Xato: "It was cold outside when I put on my warm jacket."
✅ To'g'ri: "It was cold outside, so I put on my warm jacket."
💡 Qoida: Sovuq bo'lgani sababli, SHUNING UCHUN kiyindim. Bu yerda mantiqiy bog'lovchi 'so' bo'lishi kerak.
❌ Xato: "She sliped while she was running on the grass."
✅ To'g'ri: "She slipped while she was running on the grass."
💡 Qoida: Imlo qoidasi: 'slip' bir bo'g'inli fe'l, unli + undosh bilan tugaganida oxirgi harf ikkilanadi: 'slipped'.
❌ Xato: "What did you doing when the lights went out?"
✅ To'g'ri: "What were you doing when the lights went out?"
💡 Qoida: O'sha paytda nima qilayotgan edingiz? Jarayon so'ralganda 'did you doing' emas, 'were you doing' bo'ladi.
❌ Xato: "I was hearing a loud noise while I was sleeping."
✅ To'g'ri: "I heard a loud noise while I was sleeping."
💡 Qoida: 'Hear' holat/sezgi fe'li, continuous shaklda ishlatilmaydi: 'heard' bo'ladi.
❌ Xato: "While my mother cooked, I was cleaning my room."
✅ To'g'ri: "While my mother was cooking, I was cleaning my room."
💡 Qoida: Ikki bir vaqtda davom etayotgan parallel jarayon: ikkalasi ham Past Continuous ('was cooking' & 'was cleaning').
❌ Xato: "The bell rang, while everyone stopped writing."
✅ To'g'ri: "The bell rang, so everyone stopped writing. (yoki: When the bell rang, everyone stopped writing.)"
💡 Qoida: Qo'ng'iroq chalindi, shuning uchun hamma yozishni to'xtatdi (natija: 'so').
❌ Xato: "I droped my keys when I was getting into the car."
✅ To'g'ri: "I dropped my keys when I was getting into the car."
💡 Qoida: Imlo: 'drop' so'ziga -ed qo'shilganda 'p' harfi ikkilanadi: 'dropped'.

🌐 O'zbekchadan Inglizchaga Tarjima (10 ta Gap)

Quyidagi gaplarni Past Simple, Past Continuous, when, while va so vositalaridan foydalanib ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling.

1. Men kitob o'qiyotgan edim, birdan eshik taqilladi.
Maslahat: was reading ... when ... knocked
2. Ular tushlik qilayotgan paytda, telefon jiringladi.
Maslahat: While they were having / eating ... rang
3. Kecha soat 9 da siz nima qilayotgan edingiz?
Maslahat: What were you doing at 9 ...?
4. U qahva to'kib yubordi, shuning uchun yangi ko'ylak kiydi.
Maslahat: spilled ... so she put on ...
5. Men ko'chani kesib o'tayotganimda itni ko'rdim.
Maslahat: While I was crossing ... I saw ...
6. Oyim ovqat pishirayotgan paytda, dadam yangiliklar tomosha qilayotgan edi.
Maslahat: While ... was cooking, ... was watching
7. Yomg'ir juda kuchli yog'ayotgan edi, shuning uchun biz uyda qoldik.
Maslahat: It was raining ... so we stayed ...
8. U ko'p xatolar qildi, chunki u darsga e'tibor bermayotgan edi.
Maslahat: made many mistakes ... wasn't paying attention
9. Qizaloq sirpanib ketdi va baland ovozda yig'ladi.
Maslahat: slipped and cried loudly (Past Simple ketma-ket harakatlar)
10. Siz qo'ng'iroq qilganingizda men uxlayotgan edim.
Maslahat: I was sleeping when you called
