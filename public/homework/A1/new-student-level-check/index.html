4-Qism: O'qib Tushunish / Reading Comprehension (6 ta Savol)

Ikkita qisqa matnni o'qing va ostidagi savollarga to'g'ri javobni tanlang.

📖 Matn 1: Sardor's Daily Routine (A1)
Sardor is 22 years old and he lives in Samarkand. He works in a small IT company as a junior graphic designer. Every morning, he wakes up at 6:30 AM, drinks a cup of green tea, and goes for a short run in the park. He starts work at 9:00 AM and finishes at 6:00 PM. In the evenings, he is learning English online because he wants to find a remote job in an international company next year. On weekends, he usually visits his grandparents or plays football with his friends.
1. What does Sardor do in the IT company?
2. Why is Sardor learning English online?
3. What does he do on weekends?
📖 Matn 2: A Weekend Trip to the Mountains (A1+/A2)
Last month, Nilufar and her family decided to spend the weekend in Chimgan mountains. The weather was sunny and warm when they arrived on Saturday morning. While her father was pitching the tent, Nilufar and her younger brother were collecting dry wood for the fire. Suddenly, dark clouds covered the sky and heavy rain started. They ran into a wooden cabin nearby. Although it was raining heavily outside, they were drinking hot tea and telling funny stories inside. Nilufar said it was the most memorable trip of her summer.
4. What was Nilufar's father doing when they arrived?
5. What did the family do when heavy rain started?
6. How did Nilufar describe the trip?

5-Qism: Tarjima Drilli (5 ta Gap)

Quyidagi o'zbekcha gaplarni ingliz tiliga tarjima qilib yozing.

1. Mening opam har kuni soat 8 da ishga boradi.
Foydali so'zlar: sister, every day, go to work, at 8 o'clock
2. Kecha men do'stimni ko'rdim va biz birga kitob o'qidik.
Foydali so'zlar: yesterday, see (saw), friend, read (read) books together
3. Ular hozir oshxonada tushlik tayyorlashmoqda.
Foydali so'zlar: they, right now, in the kitchen, prepare/cook lunch
4. Siz ilgari hech qachon samolyotda uchganmisiz?
Foydali so'zlar: have you ever, fly (flown) by plane, before
5. Biz kelasi yozda oilamiz bilan Antalyaga bormoqchimiz.
Foydali so'zlar: next summer, be going to go to Antalya, with family

6-Qism: O'zingiz Haqida Qisqa Insho (Self-Introduction)

Ingliz tilida o'zingiz haqingizda 5–8 ta gap yozing. Qancha ko'p yozsangiz, ustozingiz yozma darajangizni shuncha aniq baholay oladi.

💡 Yordamchi savollar:
• What is your name and where are you from? (Ismingiz va qayerdansiz?)
• What do you do? (Kasbingiz yoki o'qishingiz?)
• What do you like doing in your free time? (Bo'sh vaqtingizda nima qilasiz?)
• What did you do yesterday? (Kecha nima qildingiz?)
• Why do you want to learn English? (Nega ingliz tilini o'rganmoqchisiz?)
