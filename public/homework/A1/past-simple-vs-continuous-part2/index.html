Past Simple vs Past Continuous: Chuqurlashtirilgan Amaliyot (2-Qism)

Zamonlar o'rtasidagi farqni matn, dialog va real vaziyatlarda xatosiz qo'llash: 34 ta fe'l, 15 ta yangi so'z va 60 ta interaktiv topshiriq.

📖 Dars Qoidalari: Zamonlarni Tanlash Sxemasi

Har doim o'zingizga quyidagi 2 ta savolni bering:

⚡ 1. Harakat tugallanganmi yoki jarayonmi?

• Agar harakat o'tmishda boshlanib, yakunlangan bo'lsa ➔ Past Simple (V2).
• Agar o'sha paytda ayni davom etayotgan jarayon bo'lsa ➔ Past Continuous (was/were + V-ing).
✅ I watched the movie yesterday. (Ko'rib bo'ldim)
✅ I was watching the movie at 8 PM. (Soat 8 da ko'rayotgan edim)

⏱️ 2. Parallel harakatlar (While vs While)

Ikkita odam bir vaqtning o'zida ikkita harakatni bajarayotgan bo'lsa, ikkalasi ham Past Continuous bo'ladi:
✅ While I was studying, my brother was playing games.
✅ While mother was cooking, father was reading.

🚀 3. Ketma-ket voqealar zanjiri

Biri tugab ikkinchisi boshlangan voqealar ketma-ketligida faqat Past Simple ishlatiladi:
✅ I heard a sound, stood up, and opened the door.

💎 15 ta Yangi Mavzuviy So'z (2-Qism)

Kundalik hayot, sayohat va muloqot uchun juda qulay va sodda yangi so'zlar.

2. fly verb (flew, flown)

3. lose verb (lost, lost)

10. hurt verb (hurt, hurt)

🔍 7. Xatolarni Topish & Tahlil Qilish (10 ta)

Amaliyotda eng ko'p uchraydigan 10 ta grammatik xato va ularning yechimi.

❌ Xato: "While I was study, the telephone rang."
✅ To'g'ri: "While I was studying, the telephone rang."
💡 Izoh: 'was' dan keyin fe'lga '-ing' qo'shiladi: was studying.
❌ Xato: "I was see a terrible accident yesterday morning."
✅ To'g'ri: "I saw a terrible accident yesterday morning."
💡 Izoh: 'See' holat/qisqa fe'l, oddiy o'tgan zamonda 'saw' bo'ladi.
❌ Xato: "He broke his leg while he played football."
✅ To'g'ri: "He broke his leg while he was playing football."
💡 Izoh: 'While' dan keyin davomiy jarayon (Past Continuous) qo'yilishi shart.
❌ Xato: "Were you slept when I called you?"
✅ To'g'ri: "Were you sleeping when I called you?"
💡 Izoh: Past Continuous so'rog'ida fe'l V-ing shaklida bo'ladi: were you sleeping.
❌ Xato: "She didn't was at home yesterday at 5 PM."
✅ To'g'ri: "She wasn't at home yesterday at 5 PM."
💡 Izoh: 'To be' fe'lining o'tgan zamon inkori: wasn't / weren't ('didn't was' deb bo'lmaydi).
❌ Xato: "When we were have dinner, a bird flew into the room."
✅ To'g'ri: "When we were having dinner, a bird flew into the room."
💡 Izoh: 'were' dan keyin 'having' bo'ladi.
❌ Xato: "I was wanting to buy that bag yesterday."
✅ To'g'ri: "I wanted to buy that bag yesterday."
💡 Izoh: 'Want' holat fe'li bo'lib, -ing olmaydi: wanted.
❌ Xato: "While mother cooked, father was reading."
✅ To'g'ri: "While mother was cooking, father was reading."
💡 Izoh: Parallel uzoq harakatlar: was cooking va was reading.
❌ Xato: "He was drive very fast when the police stopped him."
✅ To'g'ri: "He was driving very fast when the police stopped him."
💡 Izoh: 'was driving' to'g'ri shakl.
❌ Xato: "It was raining so we tooked an umbrella."
✅ To'g'ri: "It was raining so we took an umbrella."
💡 Izoh: 'take' noto'g'ri fe'l, o'tgan zamoni 'took' ('tooked' deb yozilmaydi).

• Kamida 4 ta Past Simple (flew, dropped, caught, lost...)
