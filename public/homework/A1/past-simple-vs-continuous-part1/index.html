Past Simple vs Past Continuous: Qiyosiy Amaliyot (1-Qism)

O'tgan oddiy zamon (aniq, tugallangan voqealar) va o'tgan davomli zamon (jarayon, to'satdan bo'lgan hodisalar)ni farqlash hamda to'g'ri qo'llash bo'yicha to'liq mustahkamlash.

📖 Dars Qoidalari & Oltin Formulalar

Past Simple va Past Continuous o'rtasidagi asosiy farqlarni bir qarashda o'rganing.

⚡ 1. Past Simple (O'tgan Oddiy)

Formula: Subject + V2 (-ed / irregular)
Mohiyati: O'tmishda tugallangan, aniq nuqtadagi qisqa harakatlar yoki ketma-ket sodir bo'lgan voqealar.
✅ I woke up, washed my face and had breakfast.
✅ Yesterday at 3 PM, she called me.

⏱️ 2. Past Continuous (O'tgan Davomli)

Formula: was / were + Verb-ing
Mohiyati: O'tmishdagi ma'lum bir daqiqada davom etayotgan jarayon yoki fon holati.
✅ At 8:00 PM yesterday, I was studying.
✅ It was raining the whole morning.

🔗 3. When & While Bog'lovchilari

While + Past Continuous: Uzoq harakat oldidan keladi.
When + Past Simple: Kutilmagan qisqa harakat oldidan keladi.
✅ While I was cooking, the phone rang.
✅ I was sleeping when my friend knocked on the door.

⚠️ O'zbek Tili O'quvchilari Uchun 3 Katta Qopqon:

  • 1-Qopqon: Holat fe'llariga (know, want, need, understand, like) -ing qo'shmang! (❌ I was knowing ➔ ✅ I knew).
  • 2-Qopqon: 'While' dan keyin oddiygina Past Simple qo'yib yubormang (❌ While I walked ➔ ✅ While I was walking).
  • 3-Qopqon: Ketma-ket kelgan qisqa ishlarda Past Continuous ishlatmang (❌ I was opening the door and came in ➔ ✅ I opened the door and came in).

💎 15 ta Yangi Mavzuviy So'z (Sodda & Qulay)

Kundalik vaziyatlar, to'satdan bo'ladigan voqealar va Past Continuous amaliyoti uchun oson so'zlar.

1. arrive verb
yetib kelmoq
"The bus arrived while we were waiting at the station."
2. shout verb
baqirmoq, hayqirmoq
"Someone shouted my name when I was walking home."
3. fall verb (fell, fallen)
yiqilmoq, tushmoq
"The boy fell off his bike while he was riding fast."
4. ring verb (rang, rung)
jiringlamoq
"The bell rang loudly while the teacher was talking."
5. search verb
qidirmoq, izlamoq
"I was searching for my keys when my friend found them."
6. dark adj
qorong'i
"It was getting dark outside, so we went home."
7. heavy adj
og'ir, kuchli (yomg'ir)
"It was raining heavy, so we took a taxi."
8. carefully adv
ehtiyotkorlik bilan
"She was carrying the hot tea very carefully."
9. suddenly adv
to'satdan, kutilmaganda
"Suddenly, a black cat ran across the street."
10. outside adv
tashqarida
"The children were playing outside when it started to snow."
11. noise noun
shovqin, sas
"We heard a strange noise while we were studying."
12. kitchen noun
oshxona
"My mother was cooking soup in the kitchen."
13. street noun
ko'cha
"Cars were moving slowly along the wet street."
14. pocket noun
cho'ntak
"He put the money into his pocket and left."
15. story noun
hikoya, ertak
"The grandfather was telling an interesting story."

🔍 7. Xatolarni Topish & Tahlil Qilish (10 ta)

Quyidagi gaplarda o'quvchilar eng ko'p qiladigan 10 ta xato va ularning to'g'ri yechimi.

❌ Xato: "I was knowing the answer yesterday."
✅ To'g'ri: "I knew the answer yesterday."
💡 Izoh: 'Know' holat fe'li bo'lib, davomli zamonda (-ing) ishlatilmaydi.
❌ Xato: "While I walked home, it was starting to rain."
✅ To'g'ri: "While I was walking home, it started to rain."
💡 Izoh: 'While' dan keyin uzoq harakat (was walking), to'satdan boshlangan ish esa Past Simple (started).
❌ Xato: "She was cook dinner when her husband arrived."
✅ To'g'ri: "She was cooking dinner when her husband arrived."
💡 Izoh: Past Continuous da fe'lga albatta '-ing' qo'shiladi (was cooking).
❌ Xato: "What did you doing yesterday at 6 PM?"
✅ To'g'ri: "What were you doing yesterday at 6 PM?"
💡 Izoh: Aniq vaqtdagi davomiy harakat so'rog'i: 'What were you doing...?'
❌ Xato: "He was drive his car when he saw an accident."
✅ To'g'ri: "He was driving his car when he saw an accident."
💡 Izoh: 'was' dan keyin 'driving' bo'lishi kerak.
❌ Xato: "They wasn't playing football yesterday afternoon."
✅ To'g'ri: "They weren't playing football yesterday afternoon."
💡 Izoh: 'They' ko'plik olmoshi uchun 'weren't' ishlatiladi.
❌ Xato: "I was slip on the wet floor and fell down."
✅ To'g'ri: "I slipped on the wet floor and fell down."
💡 Izoh: Ketma-ket qisqa harakatlar Past Simple da aytiladi: 'slipped and fell'.
❌ Xato: "When the phone rang, I answered it while I took notes."
✅ To'g'ri: "When the phone rang, I answered it."
💡 Izoh: Telefon jiringlaganda qisqa harakat bilan javob berildi.
❌ Xato: "She was needing help so I helped her."
✅ To'g'ri: "She needed help so I helped her."
💡 Izoh: 'Need' stative verb bo'lgani uchun 'was needing' xato, 'needed' to'g'ri.
❌ Xato: "We were having dinner when suddenly someone knocks on the door."
✅ To'g'ri: "We were having dinner when suddenly someone knocked on the door."
💡 Izoh: O'tgan zamonda to'satdan bo'lgan harakat Past Simple bo'ladi: 'knocked'.
