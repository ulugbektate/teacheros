Stative vs Dynamic Verbs & 'Need' Qolipi

Holat va harakat fe'llari farqi hamda 'need + ot' va 'need to + fe'l' qoliplari

💡 Grammatika Qoidasi va Asosiy Mohiyat:

1. Holat (Stative) vs Harakat (Dynamic) fe'llari:
• Harakat fe'llari (eat, drink, play, run) $\rightarrow$ hozir bo'layotgan ishda -ing oladi (He is eating).
• Holat fe'llari (like, want, know, understand, need) $\rightarrow$ tuyg'u va fikr bo'lgani uchun -ing OLMAYDI (I know ✅, I am knowing ❌).

need + ot (noun) ➔ Biror narsaga ehtiyoj: "I need a pen." / "She needs water."
need + to + fe'l (verb) ➔ Biror ishni qilishga ehtiyoj: "I need to sleep." / "He needs to eat."

⚠️ Fe'l kelsa, albatta 'to' qo'yiladi: "I need to drink water" (NOT "I need drink" ❌).

🌐 3-Bo'lim: 10 ta Tarjima (O'zbekcha ➔ Inglizcha)

Quyidagi gaplarni mavzu qoidalariga rioya qilgan holda to'liq ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling:

1. Menga ruchka kerak. (need + noun)
2. U (qiz bola) uxlashi kerak. (need to + verb)
3. Siz darsni tushunyapsizmi? (stative: understand)
4. Bizga suv kerak. (need + noun)
5. Men bu so'zni bilaman. (stative: know)
6. Ular ovqat yeyishlari kerak. (need to + verb)
7. Men ochman, menga ovqat kerak. (hungry / need + noun)
8. Akam charchagan, u uxlashi kerak. (tired / need to sleep)
9. Menga biroz pul kerak. (need + noun)
10. Sizga yordam kerakmi? (Do you need...)
