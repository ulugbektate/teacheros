Present Simple: Future Meaning (Jadvallar & Vaqtlar)

Poyezdlar, samolyotlar, filmlar, darslar va rasmiy ish vaqtlari uchun kelasi zamon ma'nosi

💡 Grammatika Qoidasi va Asosiy Mohiyat:

Ingliz tilida rasmiy jadvallar (timetables), transport qatnovi va belgilangan umumiy vaqtlar uchun Present Simple ishlatiladi (shaxsiy niyat yoki spontan qarorlar uchun EMAS!).

Formula: I / You / We / They + V1   He / She / It + V1 + s/es

✅ "The train leaves at 08:30 tomorrow." (Poyezd ertaga 8:30 da jo'naydi — rasmiy jadval)
✅ "What time does the film start tonight?" (Film bugun kechqurun nechada boshlanadi?)
❌ "The train will leave at 08:30 tomorrow." (Jadval uchun 'will' ishlatilmaydi)

Vazifa topshirilganda natijangiz to'g'ridan-to'g'ri ustozingizning Telegramiga boradi.

📖 15 ta Yangi Mavzu Lug'ati (A1-A2 Daraja)

Mavzuni to'liq tushunish va vazifalarni mustahkam bajarish uchun ushbu 15 ta kalit so'zni o'rganing:

timetable noun
harakat/dars jadvali
"Check the train timetable before you leave."
schedule noun
kun tartibi, reja jadvali
"The bus is running on schedule."
depart verb
jo'nab ketmoq
"The morning train departs at 07:00."
arrive verb
yetib kelmoq
"What time does our flight arrive?"
platform noun
perron, platforma
"The Samarkand train leaves from platform 2."
passenger noun
yo'lovchi
"Passengers must show their tickets."
flight noun
parvoz, reys
"My flight lands in Istanbul at 14:30."
ticket office noun
chiptaxona
"Buy your pass at the ticket office."
delay verb / noun
kechikmoq, kechikish
"There is a 20-minute delay today."
station noun
vokzal, bekat
"Meet me at the central station."
board verb
(transportga) chiqmoq
"We board the plane at 10:15."
gate noun
chiqish eshigi (aeroportda)
"Go to gate 4 for your flight."
luggage noun
yuk, bagaj
"Take your luggage to the counter."
journey noun
sayohat, yo'l
"Have a safe journey to Bukhara!"
cancel verb
bekor qilmoq
"The evening train is canceled."

🎯 2-Bo'lim: 10 ta Tanlov Testi (Grammatika & Nozikliklar)

5. The autumn term ______ on September 2nd.
6. When ______ the supermarket ______ tomorrow morning?

8. Hurry up! The museum ______ in twenty minutes.
9. Tomorrow ______ Sunday, so the banks are closed.
10. What time ______ our flight ______ at the gate?
