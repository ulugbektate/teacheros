# 📘 Present Simple: Katta Amaliy Uyga Vazifa (Beginner A0 → A1)
**O'quvchi uchun alohida vazifalar to'plami**  
**Asos:** Raymond Murphy — *Essential Grammar in Use* (Units 5, 6, 7)  
**Format:** Barcha mashqlar bo'limlarga bo'linmasdan, bir o'tirishda ketma-ket to'liq bajariladi.

---

## 🎯 Darsning Asosiy Qoidalari (Quick Murphy Summary)
1. **Darak gaplar (Positive):**
   * `I / You / We / They` + **V1** (fe'lning asil shakli). *Masalan: I work in Tashkent.*
   * `He / She / It` + **V + -s / -es / -ies**. *Masalan: She works, He watches, She studies, He has.*
2. **Inkor gaplar (Negative):**
   * `I / You / We / They` + **don't + V1**. *Masalan: We don't watch TV.*
   * `He / She / It` + **doesn't + V1** *(fe'ldagi -s tushib qoladi!)*. *Masalan: He doesn't like meat.*
3. **Savol gaplar (Questions):**
   * **Do** + `I / you / we / they` + **V1**? *Masalan: Do you speak English?*
   * **Does** + `he / she / it` + **V1**? *Masalan: Does she live in Samarkand?*

## 💬 5-MASHQ: Qisqa javoblar (Short Answers)
Savollarga to'g'ri qisqa javobni tanlang:
1. "Do you like pizza?" ➔ (Yes, I do. / Yes, I like. / Yes, I am.)
2. "Does your brother work in a bank?" ➔ (No, he doesn't. / No, he not work. / No, he isn't.)
3. "Do they speak Russian?" ➔ (No, they don't. / No, they doesn't. / No, they not.)
4. "Does Malika study at school?" ➔ (Yes, she does. / Yes, she studies. / Yes, she is.)
5. "Do you live in Bukhara?" ➔ (No, I don't. / No, I not live. / No, I'm not.)
6. "Does it rain in winter?" ➔ (Yes, it does. / Yes, it rains. / Yes, it is.)
