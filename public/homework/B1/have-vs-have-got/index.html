Have vs Have Got: Possession, Actions & Differences

Britaniya va Amerika inglizchasi farqi, o'tgan zamon tuzog'i va iboraviy harakatlar (have breakfast, have a shower)

💡 Grammatika Qoidasi va Asosiy Mohiyat:

1. Egalik, oilaviy munosabatlar va kasalliklar:
• Britaniya (UK): have got / has got $\rightarrow$ "I've got a car." / "Have you got a car?" / "I haven't got a car."
• Amerika (US): have / has $\rightarrow$ "I have a car." / "Do you have a car?" / "I don't have a car."

MUHIM 1: O'tgan zamonda 'have got' MUMKIN EMAS!
O'tmishda egalik uchun faqat had ishlatiladi: "When I was young, I had a bike" ✅ (NOT "I had got a bike" ❌).

MUHIM 2: Iboraviy harakatlarda 'have got' QAT'IYAN ISHLATILMAYDI!
Ovqatlanish, dush, dam olish, sayohat kabilarda faqat have keladi va -ing olishi mumkin:
• have breakfast / lunch / dinner (NOT have got breakfast ❌)
• have a shower / bath (NOT have got a shower ❌)
• "I am having lunch right now." ✅

🌐 3-Bo'lim: 10 ta Tarjima (O'zbekcha ➔ Inglizcha)

Quyidagi gaplarni mavzu qoidalariga rioya qilgan holda to'liq ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling:

1. Mening yangi noutbukim bor. (have got / have bilan yozing)
2. Sizda ortiqcha qalam bormi? (Have you got / Do you have)
3. Uning (qiz bola) kuchli bosh og'rig'i bor. (headache)
4. Men har kuni ertalab soat 7 da dush qabul qilaman. (have a shower - iboraviy harakat!)
5. Kecha biz bayramda juda maroqli vaqt o'tkazdik. (had a great time)
6. Ular o'tgan yili katta hovliga ega edilar. (had - o'tgan zamonda have got MUMKIN EMAS!)
7. Biz hozir restoranda tushlik qilyapmiz, keyinroq qo'ng'iroq qiling. (having lunch)
8. Mening hech qanday naqd pulim yo'q. (haven't got / don't have)
9. Uning ikkita akasi va bitta singlisi bor. (siblings)
10. Ertaga soat 3 da shifokor bilan uchrashuvim (qabulim) bor. (have an appointment)
