🚀 Future Perfect Continuous (Kengaytirilgan Master Kurs)

Kelajakdagi ma'lum bir nuqtaga qadar davom etgan jarayon, stative fe'llar tuzog'i va amaliy ko'nikmalar.

90+ Amaliy Topshiriqlar

📌 1. Future Perfect Continuous Formulalari

Kelajakdagi ma'lum bir vaqt nuqtasiga qadar harakat qancha vaqtdan beri davom etayotganini ko'rsatadi.

(+) S + will have been + V-ing

"By 2030, I will have been working here for 10 years."
(-) S + will not (won't) have been + V-ing

"We won't have been waiting for long when he arrives."
(?) Will + S + have been + V-ing?

"How long will you have been living abroad by next year?"

⚡ 2. FPC vs FP Simple (Natija vs Davomiylik)

Future Perfect Simple: Nechta ish bitganligi / yakunlangan natija (How many / how much).

✅ "By midnight, she will have written five chapters." (5 ta bob tayyor bo'ladi!)

Future Perfect Continuous: Ish qancha vaqt to'xtovsiz davom etganligi (How long).

✅ "By midnight, she will have been writing for eight hours." (8 soatdan beri yozayotgan bo'ladi!)
⚠️ Stative Verbs Qopqoni: know, believe, belong, own, understand, like fe'llari HECH QACHON continuous shaklida kelmaydi! Faqat Simple: "will have known" (will have been knowing EMAS!).
