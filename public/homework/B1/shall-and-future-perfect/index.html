Modal Verb "Shall" & Future Perfect Simple

Ushbu vazifada siz Shall modal fe'lining amaliy qo'llanishi (taklif, rasmiy qoidalar, qat'iyat) hamda Future Perfect Simple (kelajakdagi ma'lum nuqtaga qadar tugallanadigan ishlar) mavzularini chuqur o'zlashtirasiz.

⚖️ 1. Modal Verb: SHALL

• Shall I...? — Yordam yoki xizmat taklif qilish: "Shall I open the window?" (Oynani ochaymi?)

• Shall we...? — Birgalikda nimadir qilishni taklif qilish: "Where shall we meet?" (Qayerda uchrashamiz?)

• Rasmiy burch / Shartnomalar: "The tenant shall pay rent on time." (Ijara to'lashga majbur).

• Qat'iy ont / Muqarrar kelajak: "We shall overcome." (Biz albatta g'alaba qozonamiz).

Shall I / Shall we + V1 (asl fe'l)
⚠️ O'zbekcha tuzoq: Taklif berganda "Will I help you?" deyilmaydi! Faqat "Shall I help you?" to'g'ri.

⏱️ 2. Future Perfect Simple

• Kelajakdagi ma'lum bir vaqtdan oldin to'liq tugallanadigan ish-harakatlar uchun ishlatiladi.

• Kalit vaqt belgilari: by then, by tomorrow, by 2030, by the end of this year, in two weeks' time.

• By the time + Present Simple: "By the time you arrive, I will have cooked dinner."

Subject + will have + V3 (Past Participle)
⚠️ O'zbekcha tuzoq: "By the time you will come" ❌ emas, "By the time you COME" ✅ bo'ladi!

💡 Siz yozgan barcha javoblar brauzeringizda avtomatik saqlanib boradi.

20 Ta Xatoni Topish va Tuzatish (Error Hunt Drill)

Quyidagi gaplarda o'zbek o'quvchilari ko'p qiladigan tipik xatolar bor. Gapni to'g'rilab yozing:

1. ❌ Will I open the window for you? It's very hot.

💡 Maslahat: Taklif berishda 'Will I' emas, 'Shall I' ishlatiladi.

2. ❌ Shall he to sign the contract right now?

💡 Maslahat: Modal fe'llardan keyin 'to' zarrachasi ishlatilmaydi.

3. ❌ What will we do tonight? Let's decide together.

💡 Maslahat: Birgalikda taklif qilishda 'What shall we do?' to'g'ri.

4. ❌ Will I make you a cup of coffee, sir?

💡 Maslahat: O'zbekcha 'Kofe damlab beraymi?' taklifida 'Shall I' qo'llaniladi.

5. ❌ Let's go to the museum, will we?

💡 Maslahat: 'Let's...' ning tag question shakli doim 'shall we?' bo'ladi.

6. ❌ The buyer shall pays the fee upon arrival.

💡 Maslahat: 'shall' modal fe'lidan keyin fe'l qo'shimchasiz asosiy shaklda keladi.

7. ❌ Shall we going for a walk after lunch?

💡 Maslahat: 'Shall we' dan keyin fe'lning V1 shakli (go) keladi, -ing emas.

8. ❌ Where will we meet tomorrow? What is your suggestion?

💡 Maslahat: Maslahat va taklifda 'Where shall we meet?' to'g'ri.

9. ❌ Passengers shall not smoking inside the cabin.

💡 Maslahat: Modal fe'l + not + V1: 'shall not smoke'.

10. ❌ Will I carry this box for you?

💡 Maslahat: Yordam taklifida 'Shall I carry' to'g'ri.

11. ❌ By 2030, I will graduate from university.

💡 Maslahat: 'By 2030' borligi uchun Future Simple emas, Future Perfect kerak.

12. ❌ By the time you will come, I will have finished dinner.

💡 Maslahat: 'By the time' dan keyin kelajak zamonda 'will' ishlatilmaydi, Present Simple ('come') bo'ladi.

13. ❌ She will has written the entire report by 5 PM.

💡 Maslahat: Future Perfectda egadan qat'i nazar 'will have' bo'ladi, 'will has' mavjud emas.

14. ❌ By next summer, they will have build a new hospital.

💡 Maslahat: Fe'lning 3-shakli V3 ishlatilishi shart: build -> built.

15. ❌ In two years' time, we will have be friends for a decade.

💡 Maslahat: be fe'lining V3 shakli 'been' dir: 'will have been'.

16. ❌ By tomorrow morning, the rain will stopped.

💡 Maslahat: 'have' yordamchi fe'li tushirib qoldirilgan: 'will have stopped'.

17. ❌ By the time the doctor will arrive, the patient will have recovered.

💡 Maslahat: 'By the time' dan keyin 'arrives' (Present Simple) bo'ladi.

18. ❌ He will have finish the marathon before noon.

💡 Maslahat: Fe'l V3 shaklida bo'lishi shart: 'finished'.

19. ❌ By December, I will have learn 500 new English words.

💡 Maslahat: learn fe'li V3 shakliga o'tishi kerak: 'learned'.

20. ❌ By Friday, will you completed the assignment?

💡 Maslahat: Future Perfect so'rog'ida 'have' bo'lishi shart: 'will you have completed'.

Kengaytirilgan Ijodiy Yozma Vazifalar (Writing Section)

Ikkala mavzuni real hayotiy kontekstda erkin qo'llang:

✍️ 1-Vazifa: "The Official Meeting Protocol & Proposals" (Shall amaliyoti)

Tasavvur qiling, siz xalqaro tashkilot yoki kompaniyada uchrashuv o'tkazmoqdasiz. Kamida 4 marta 'Shall I...?' va 'Shall we...?' hamda 2 marta rasmiy qoida (shall) ishlatib, 6-8 ta gapdan iborat dialog yoki protokol yozing.

🚀 2-Vazifa: "My Life & Career Projections by 2030" (Future Perfect amaliyoti)

2030-yilga qadar hayotingizda qanday marralarni zabt etgan bo'lasiz? Kamida 5 ta 'will have + V3' va 'by the time' iborasini ishlatib, 8-10 ta gapdan iborat kelajak reja inshosini yozing.

❓ Ustozga Savolingiz Yoki Fikringiz:

Ushbu 2 ta mavzuda sizga eng qiyin tuyulgan nuqta nima bo'ldi? Qaysi so'roq yoki qoidaga ko'proq mashq kerak?
