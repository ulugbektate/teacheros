Used to vs Would: Past Habits & States

O'tmishdagi odatlar, nostalgiya, takroriy harakatlar va holat fe'llari (stative verbs) farqi

💡 Grammatika Qoidasi va Asosiy Mohiyat:

O'tmishdagi voqea va odatlarni ifodalashda quyidagi qat'iy farqlarni yodda tuting:

used to + V1 ➔ Odatlar (actions) HAMDA O'tmishdagi holatlar (states: live, have, be, like, know, own).
would + V1 ➔ FAQAT takroriy harakatlar (actions) va nostalgiya uchun! Holat fe'llari bilan 'would' MUMKIN EMAS!

✅ "I used to live in Samarkand." (To'g'ri — live holat fe'li)
❌ "I would live in Samarkand." (XATO! 'would' bilan holat fe'li kelmaydi)
✅ "Every Sunday, grandma would bake us a pie." (To'g'ri — takroriy harakat)
⚠️ Bir marta bo'lgan aniq ish uchun (yesterday, in 2020) faqat Past Simple ishlatiladi!

📖 15 ta Yangi Mavzu Lug'ati (B1 O'rta Daraja)

Mavzuni to'liq tushunish va vazifalarni mustahkam bajarish uchun ushbu 15 ta kalit so'zni o'rganing:

memory noun
xotira
"I have so many happy memories of my childhood."
childhood noun
bolalik davri
"In my childhood, we played outside until dark."
habit noun
odat
"Waking up early used to be his daily habit."
grow up phr verb
ulg'aymoq, voyaga yetmoq
"I grew up in a quiet town with my cousins."
spend time phrase
vaqt o'tkazmoq
"We would spend time together every evening."
neighborhood noun
mahalla, atrof-muhit
"I used to know every child in our neighborhood."
gather verb
to'planmoq, yig'ilmoq
"Our family would gather on Sunday afternoons."
relative noun
qarindosh
"During holidays, all our relatives would visit us."
regularly adverb
muntazam ravishda, doimiy
"He would regularly play chess with his grandfather."
remember verb
eslamoq, yodda tutmoq
"Do you remember the games we used to play?"
change verb / noun
o'zgarmoq, o'zgarish
"Our old street has changed so much over the years."
share verb
bo'lishmoq, birga ishlatmoq
"My brother and I used to share a small bedroom."
prefer verb
afzal ko'rmoq (holat fe'li)
"She used to prefer tea, but now she drinks coffee."
belong to phr verb
tegishli bo'lmoq (holat fe'li)
"This bicycle used to belong to my elder brother."
miss verb
sog'inmoq
"I really miss the food my grandmother used to cook."

🎯 2-Bo'lim: 10 ta Tanlov Testi (Grammatika & Nozikliklar)

Har bir savolda bitta to'g'ri variant mavjud. Variantlar (A, B, C, D) teng aralashtirilgan:

1. When I was in primary school, I ______ long curly hair.
2. Every summer, my grandfather ______ take us fishing at sunrise.
3. We ______ in a small apartment before moving to this big house.
4. ______ you ______ play the violin when you were younger?
5. Yesterday, my brother ______ his driving test on his first attempt.
6. I ______ like mushrooms, but now I eat them all the time.
7. In high school, we ______ spend hours discussing science after classes.
8. My parents ______ a red Soviet car in the 1990s.
9. Whenever it rained, we ______ stay inside and play board games.
10. Which sentence is grammatically INCORRECT?
🌐 3-Bo'lim: 10 ta Tarjima (O'zbekcha ➔ Inglizcha)

Quyidagi gaplarni mavzu qoidalariga rioya qilgan holda to'liq ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling:

1. Bolaligimda bu mahallada yashardim. (used to / would?)
2. Har yakshanba buvim bizga shirin pirog pishirib berardilar.
3. Ilgari uning uzun sochi bo'lardi, ammo hozir kalta kestirgan.
4. Siz ilgari bu universitetda ishlarmidingiz?
5. Biz yozda har kuni daryo bo'yiga borardik va suzardik.
6. Men ilgari achchiq taomlarni yoqtirmasdim, ammo hozir yaxshi ko'raman.
7. Uning oilasi shahar markazida katta uyga ega bo'lardi.
8. O'tgan yili u yangi mashina sotib oldi. (Diqqat: bir martalik ish!)
9. Kechqurunlari otam bizga qiziqarli kitoblar o'qib berardilar.
10. Biz ilgari juda yaqin do'st edik, keyinchalik aloqamiz uzildi.

✏️ 5-Bo'lim: 10 ta Bo'shliqni To'ldirish (Qavsdagi fe'llar)

Qavs ichidagi fe'lni to'g'ri shaklda bo'sh joyga qo'ying:

1. When we were kids, we ______ (spend) every summer at the lake. (both possible)
2. My uncle ______ (own) a small bookshop near the square. (only used to - state)
3. On warm evenings, father ______ (sit) outside and tell us stories. (both possible)
4. I ______ (not / like) olives when I was ten years old. (only used to)
5. ______ you ______ (believe) in tooth fairies as a child? (only used to)
6. Every morning, the old baker ______ (open) his bakery at 5 AM. (both possible)
7. They ______ (have) a black dog named Bruno. (only used to - state)
8. In secondary school, we ______ (walk) home together. (both possible)
9. My grandmother ______ (be) very strict about homework. (only used to - state)
10. He ______ (buy) a brand new laptop two days ago. (Past Simple only - single event)
🧩 6-Bo'lim: 5 ta So'zlar Tartibi (Gap Tuzing)

Aralash so'zlarni to'g'ri ketma-ketlikda joylashtirib, to'liq gap tuzing:

1. [ I / used / to / have / curly / hair / . ]
💡 Ma'nosi: Ilgari sochim jingalak bo'lardi.
2. [ Every / summer / we / would / go / camping / . ]
💡 Ma'nosi: Har yozda chodirda dam olishga borardik.
3. [ Did / you / use / to / play / chess / ? ]
💡 Ma'nosi: Ilgari shaxmat o'ynarmidingiz?
4. [ He / didn't / use / to / like / spicy / food / . ]
💡 Ma'nosi: U ilgari achchiq ovqatni yoqtirmasdi.
5. [ We / would / sit / and / talk / for / hours / . ]
💡 Ma'nosi: Biz o'tirib soatlab suhbatlashardik.
