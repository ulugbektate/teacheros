B1 O'rta Daraja

Present Simple: Future Meaning (Jadvallar & Itineraries)

Rasmiy transport qatnovi, aviakompaniya marshrutlari, anjumanlar, ko'rgazmalar va muddatlar uchun kelasi zamon ma'nosi

💡 B1 Darajasidagi Grammatik Mohiyat:

1. Rasmiy jadval (Official Timetable / Schedule): Poyezdlar, samolyotlar, filmlar, konferensiyalar, dars semestrlari va muassasalarning ish vaqtlari uchun Present Simple qat'iy standartdir (shaxsiy reja uchun emas!).

2. Qiyosiy farq (Crucial Contrast):
• "Our flight takes off at 07:15 tomorrow." $\rightarrow$ Rasmiy jadval (Present Simple).
• "I am flying to London with my manager." $\rightarrow$ Shaxsiy kelishuv (Present Continuous).
• "When the train arrives, we will call you." $\rightarrow$ Vaqt ergash gapida (when / as soon as) kelasi zamon o'rniga Present Simple.

Formula: Subject + V1 / V1+(s/es) ➔ "The flight departs..." / "Conferences start..."
So'roq: Does / Do + Subject + V1? ➔ "What time does the exhibition close?"

Vazifa topshirilganda natijangiz to'g'ridan-to'g'ri ustozingizning Telegramiga boradi.

🎯 2-Bo'lim: 10 ta Tanlov Testi (Grammatika & Nozikliklar)

Present Simple future meaning va jadvallar qoidasi bo'yicha testlar (Variantlar A, B, C, D muvozanatli taqsimlangan):

1. According to the official itinerary, our flight ______ from Terminal 1 at 07:45 tomorrow.
2. What time ______ the international tech summit ______ on Monday morning?
3. The registration deadline ______ at 23:59 tonight, so please submit your files immediately.
4. As soon as the connecting train ______ at platform 4, we will transfer our luggage.
5. Which sentence correctly demonstrates an OFFICIAL SCHEDULE rather than a personal arrangement?
6. Tomorrow ______ Saturday, which means the central administrative offices ______ closed.
7. Our high-speed train to Bukhara ______ in forty minutes, so we should head to the platform.
8. When ______ the academic semester officially ______ this winter?
9. The live broadcast ______ at 21:00 sharp and ______ for two hours.
10. I am traveling to London on Friday, and my flight ______ at Heathrow at 11:30 AM.
🌐 3-Bo'lim: 10 ta Tarjima (O'zbekcha ➔ Inglizcha)

Quyidagi jadvallar va tadbirlarga oid gaplarni to'liq ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling:

1. Bizning tranzit (bog'lanish) reysimiz ertaga soat 14:30 da jo'naydi.
2. Xalqaro texnologiya anjumani dushanba kuni soat 09:00 da boshlanadi.
3. Zamonaviy san'at ko'rgazmasi kelasi juma kuni soat 18:00 da yopiladi.
4. Poyezd 3-platformaga yetib kelganida, menga xabar bering. (when + Present Simple)
5. Konferensiyaga ro'yxatdan o'tishning oxirgi muddati bugun yarim tunda tugaydi.
6. Ertaga soat nechada bank bayram tufayli o'z eshiklarini yopadi?
7. Ertaga ertalabki ma'ruzalar soat 08:30 da boshlanadi va 13:00 da tugaydi.
8. Katta anjumanlar zali tashrif buyuruvchilar uchun soat 10:00 da ochiladi.
9. Poyezdimiz kechiktirildimi yoki jadval bo'yicha o'z vaqtida jo'naydimi?
10. Kuzgi o'quv semestri rasman 15-sentyabrda boshlanadi va dekabrda tugaydi.
